# Roll20 import audit

Objective: correct import errors and their effects on existing saved characters, without replacing current characters with their old exports or losing subsequent advancement.

## Verified repairs

- Shocking Spark, Scourge of Force and the Flesh Ripper spell now produce their missing attack/effect rolls. Spark uses 1/2/3 sparks at caster levels 1/6/10; Scourge uses one plus one per three levels, capped at four tendrils, all at the highest melee-touch bonus. Damage uses each spell's own cap; multi-attack casts spend one preparation or Factotum choice/inspiration. Native and Roll20-import regression tests retain later level-ups, HP, notes, overrides and expenditure. Metal-target Spark bonuses, criticals and Flesh Ripper bleeding remain explicitly noted for player resolution, not silently assumed. Source checks: Dragonlance Campaign Setting p.111 ([Shocking Spark](https://new.dndtools.org/spells/shocking-spark-5016)), Champions of Ruin p.34 ([Scourge of Force](https://webmail.dndtools.org/m/spells/champions-of-ruin--27/scourge-of-force--281/)), Book of Vile Darkness p.96 and bundled Spells v6.01 descriptions.
- Shout and Greater Shout use fixed ordinary-target damage (5d6 / 10d6); Greater Shout recognizes the actual `shout-greater` catalog/import ID as well as the old alias. No attack roll is added to these save-based cones. Existing effect overrides remain available for crystalline targets; this does not automatically select target-specific damage. Regression tests cover catalog IDs, caster levels, Factotum Shout, cast expenditure and native save/load without rewriting preparations, HP or notes. Sources: [Shout](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/shout.htm), [Greater Shout](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/shoutGreater.htm).
- Mass Cure/Inflict Wounds and Mass Heal now recognize the actual catalog/import IDs ending in `-mass`, while retaining the old `mass-` aliases. Cast rolls the effect once, with the correct caster-level cap, without adding touch attacks or spending per target. Manual effect overrides keep priority. Regression coverage uses all nine actual catalog entries and a Roll20-imported prayer after level-up and native save/load; no stored IDs, preparations, expenditure, HP or source data are rewritten. Sources: [Mass Cure Light Wounds](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/cureLightWoundsMass.htm), [Mass Inflict Critical Wounds](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/inflictCriticalWoundsMass.htm), [Mass Heal](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/healMass.htm).
- Spell Cast now includes detected melee/ranged attack rolls in the same practice/Beyond20 request as effect dice, including Arcane Dilettante and Vaulnaen. Scorching Ray and Splinterbolt receive separate attack/effect pairs at their caster-level breakpoints; Spiritual Weapon starts with one BAB + Wisdom attack against normal AC; Meteor Swarm separates impact from explosion dice. Post card remains roll-free. Optional per-spell delivery/count/conditional modifiers survive saves without modifying old preparations, level-ups or expenditure; Attack only spends nothing and is explicitly for legal ongoing effects/held charges. Automatic detection is conservative, not exhaustive: conditional delivery, unknown compendium formulas, special attack statistics and later-round actions still need player review. Sources: [Scorching Ray](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/scorchingRay.htm), [Spiritual Weapon](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/spiritualWeapon.htm), [Meteor Swarm](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/spells/meteorSwarm.htm), and bundled Splinterbolt source text. Local browser check: level-11 Wizard Cast produced three attacks and three 4d6 rolls while spending one prepared copy; Attack only left that expenditure unchanged. No live Roll20 delivery was attempted.
- Factotum Cunning Knowledge now validates the current class, at least one skill rank, inspiration, and daily use before generating a roll or spending resources. Duplicate same-name skill rows share the once-per-day restriction; distinct Knowledge specialties remain separate. Existing stored use IDs are honored unchanged, and encounter refresh does not clear daily uses. Failed attempts and roll previews do not spend inspiration; level-up and browser/native round-trip tests retain ranks and all other expenditure (`factotum-knowledge.test.mjs`). No skill bonus types or saved statistics were rewritten.
- Numbered divine/arcane spell rows now recognize boolean and text checkbox preparations/usage, as well as numeric counts. Checked preparation imports as one copy; checked usage consumes one copy (and contributes to spontaneous spent slots), rather than allowing a free cast. Explicit numeric zeroes and unresolved formulas retain their meanings. Existing saves receive a one-time review warning when their source contains affected checkbox values; today's counts are never replaced by historical counts or refilled. Browser/native round-trips retain later levels, HP, prepared/spent values and notes (`roll20-spell-preparation.test.mjs`).
- Silverbeard's specialty (`silverbeard`) and catalog/import (`supp-silverbeard`) IDs now share the existing Cast-triggered self effect. Both apply +2 sacred AC and conditional dwarf Diplomacy for the same caster-level duration, without stacking duplicate effects. Specialty selection recognizes an existing catalog copy and preserves its preparations, expenditure, notes and formula. Opening a save never casts it; already duplicated spell rows are not merged or deleted, and arbitrary custom names are not treated as verified spell identities (`cleric-specialty.test.mjs`).
- Factotum Arcane Dilettante's Cast button now includes the existing spell-effect dice and scaled range/duration instead of sending only a description. No-save abilities no longer show a spurious DC on their roll card. Tests cover generated Roll20 messages, single daily use, inspiration exhaustion, and a Factotum/Cleric Axe Brother level-up/load retaining separate spell resources, counters and damage (`factotum-casting.test.mjs`). Supported effect formulas still use the shared spell formula catalog; unknown formulas and target-specific outcomes are not guessed. The [Wizards 3.5 FAQ, pp. 17-18](https://www.d20pfsrd.com/wp-content/uploads/sites/12/2017/01/Main35FAQv06302008.pdf) confirms inspiration expenditure, once-per-day choices and spell-like-ability treatment.
- Selecting Axe Brother reuses an identifiable legacy Cleric book even without casting metadata. It does not create a second fresh slot pool, touch existing spent/prepared counts, or reuse domain/different-class books. Ambiguous ordinary Cleric books require review; failed selections leave the character unchanged (`cleric-specialty.test.mjs`). This preserves the existing campaign rule of Silverbeard at cleric level 1; no additional specialty rules are inferred. Previously duplicated books are not automatically deleted or merged because their later expenditure may differ.
- Modern/legacy Move Silently modifier aliases count once per modifier category, rather than being added twice. A one-time old-save repair removes only the unchanged duplicated source component, preserving later rank purchases and imported-bonus review choices. Numeric source totals, repeating-row overrides, edited components and conflicting attributes are left alone (`roll20-skill-aliases.test.mjs`).
- The Roll20 exporter now queries critical defaults and instantiated weapon components for explicitly named fixed slots 1-10. Conflicting multiplier attributes can use Roll20's queried value rather than falling back to x2. Raw duplicates remain intact and flagged. Unnamed slots are not queried, and missing modern dice defaults are not mixed into legacy dice fields (`roll20-exporter.test.mjs`). This is a future-export correction requiring the installed exporter script to be updated; old saves with unresolved conflicts are not guessed or re-imported. Mocked API coverage is not a live Roll20 game test.
- Automation adoption no longer subtracts a monk's unavailable unarmored AC bonus while armor, a shield or load blocks it. A one-time Roll20 repair compares the old miscellaneous-AC deduction with the original export, removing it only when unchanged; later levels, Wisdom changes, gear, HP and spent resources stay intact. Edited/overridden or already corrected values are left alone. An armored monk recorded at AC 14 now remains at 14 instead of dropping to 11 (`roll20-monk-defense.test.mjs`). This repair concerns the importer deduction, not a blanket rewrite of source AC to standard rules.
- Shield-use restrictions follow carried, equipped, nonzero-quantity shield gear rather than a positive AC bonus. Custom imported shields with zero/negative recorded protection no longer incorrectly allow monk flurry, fast movement or unarmored bonuses. Unequipping the item restores eligibility; a shield spell or a manual AC bonus alone is not treated as physical shield use. Runtime-only correction leaves existing gear stats and character progress unchanged (`roll20-shield-presence.test.mjs`). Checked against the [3.5 monk shield restrictions](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/classes/monk.htm) on 2026-10-21.
- Imported psionic powers retain their recorded discipline, descriptor, display, manifesting time, range, target, duration, save, resistance and augmentation checkbox in visible power notes and posted cards. The upstream `powerrange1` input is honored at every level. Existing saves recover omitted details once only for uniquely matched, unchanged notes; later costs, effect formulas, levels, focus and spent points are preserved. Edited/deleted/ambiguous powers are not overwritten or resurrected. Long details are bounded with a warning and the full raw export retained (`roll20-power-details.test.mjs`). Field names were checked against the [official Roll20 sheet](https://github.com/Roll20/roll20-character-sheets/blob/master/D%26D_3-5/charsheet_3-5.html) on 2026-10-21; macros are not executed or converted into effect formulas.
- Custom imported shield names no longer change proficiency when capitalized differently. Tower-shield classification is shared by class training, alternate-feature restrictions, and the separate -2 tower attack penalty; custom bucklers respect buckler-only/light-shield training. Recorded equipment stats, explicit proficiency overrides, material adjustments, current level-ups and resources are not rewritten (`roll20-shield-proficiency.test.mjs`). [Tower shield proficiency](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/feats.htm#towerShieldProficiency) and [Hit-and-Run's cross-class restriction](https://srd.dndtools.org/srd/classes/baseCore/fighter.html#drow-fighter) were checked on 2026-10-21.
- Weapon Finesse no longer overrides a selected Intelligence, Wisdom, Charisma or Constitution attack ability. It substitutes Dexterity only for Strength; imported and later edited alternate selectors remain intact in attacks, full attacks and critical confirmations (`roll20-attack-totals.test.mjs`). This follows [Weapon Finesse's Strength-to-Dexterity substitution](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/feats.htm#weaponFinesse); selecting another ability remains a player/DM rule choice, not a grant of a new ability substitution.
- Numeric weapon attack totals no longer gain duplicate original Weapon Focus/Greater Weapon Focus bonuses when separate source components are absent, zero or unresolved. The version-two weapon-bonus migration repairs unchanged version-one saves (including partial old Focus adjustments), preserving later levels, abilities, feats, weapon upgrades, resource use, explicit zero choices and edited misc fields. Numeric totals remain source evidence, not proof that the source followed the rules; formulas are not treated as totals (`roll20-attack-totals.test.mjs`).
- Imported class-linked casting and psionic traditions no longer remain permanently manual after automatic sheet adoption. A one-time comparison repairs unchanged profiles using numeric source offsets measured at the original level, so later class/ability advancement applies without resetting preparations, spent slots, or spent power points. Missing/formula totals are not offsets against prestige progression. Edited, removed, generic and domain profiles remain unchanged with review guidance; subsequent manual progression choices are respected. Spell-used totals are imported even when the export omits caster-level and slot-maximum fields (`roll20-casting-progression.test.mjs`).
- Pre-v2.7.9 first-level divine spells in `repeating_spells` (`spellname11`, `spellprep11`, `spellused11`) are no longer dropped. Fresh imports retain preparation/use counts. Roll20's copied `spells11` row takes precedence by case-insensitive RowID, preventing duplicates. Existing saves recover omitted entries only into a unique matching tradition, with zero new preparations and no changes to current slot usage; source counts remain in recovery notes. Manual re-additions, later class/spell changes, and deletion after the one-time repair are respected (`roll20-legacy-spells.test.mjs`). The retired section and migration precedence were verified against the upstream Roll20 sheet on 2026-10-21.
- Numeric global attack/damage adjustments and bounded global extra-damage dice are imported into Combat > Global weapon modifiers, including the source enabled flag. They affect weapon rolls only, work in manual and automatic modes, and extra dice are not multiplied on criticals. Numeric attack totals get a separate included-component offset so globals count once; complete damage formulas skip global damage until reviewed. Old saves with unchanged weapon components gain the settings without losing later advancement/upgrades/resources. Possible manual compensation (changed weapon components, active attack/damage effects, or a situational modifier) remains unchanged with an explicit review warning. Exporter core queries now include these fields (`roll20-global-combat.test.mjs`, `roll20-exporter.test.mjs`).
- Explicit weapon enhancement components now retain their bonus type during automatic calculations, so a +2 imported weapon and a +3 enhancement effect contribute +3, not +5. Original manual misc values remain intact with separate editable imported-component offsets. Existing saves receive a one-time source comparison; later upgrades (including explicit zeroes), advancement, resources, and misc edits are respected. Linked gear receives the source enhancement only when its setting is missing; ambiguous duplicate gear is not auto-linked. Complete/unsupported damage components remain review-only (`roll20-weapon-enhancement.test.mjs`).
- Missing top-level armor summaries become functional Gear entries. Existing saves receive a one-time restoration, preserving progress and explicit equipment edits (`roll20-armor-migration.test.mjs`).
- Positive armor-check deductions normalize to negative modifiers; armor and shield penalties cannot cancel because of inconsistent signs (`roll20-regression.test.mjs`).
- `arcane_spell_failure` and `arcanespellfailure` are alternative total spell-failure fields, not modifiers to add to armor/shield values. Components are a fallback only. Existing saves with the old double-counting signature receive a one-time correction; user-edited offsets/overrides are retained for review (`roll20-spell-failure.test.mjs`).
- Explicit imported Weapon Focus and Weapon Specialization components are no longer applied twice by automation. Corrections are separate, automatic-only Combat fields; manual values remain intact. Existing saves compare the original weapon entry and keep later levels, feats, upgrades, notes, and spent resources. Changed components, replacement/duplicate identities, and explicit adjustment choices are not overwritten (`roll20-weapon-bonuses.test.mjs`). Complete formulas remain review-only where included specialization cannot be established.
- Nonstandard weapon dice and complete source damage formulas survive automatic catalog matching. A separate recorded-dice choice retains catalog proficiency/range/feat metadata. A one-time old-save repair matches retained formulas against the raw source, preserves subsequent character/weapon edits, and leaves explicit dice choices alone. Normal monk dice still progress after level-up (`roll20-weapon-dice.test.mjs`). Complex source formulas remain editable and require review after size/class changes because their internal bonus types cannot be inferred safely.

Existing imports receive skill review on normal loading, switching, and native-backup import; no Roll20 re-import is required. The source is reconstructed only for comparison, never assigned over the current character. A fixed import-time adjustment preserves later ranks, feats, ability changes, class levels, history, and spent resources. Changed misc fields require review instead of automatic correction. Choices survive browser/native round-trips and repeated loads (`tests/roll20-skill-bonuses.test.mjs`). Missing or invalid source exports leave current skill values unchanged and add a warning.

The spell-failure interpretation was checked against the upstream [Roll20 D&D 3.5 sheet](https://github.com/Roll20/roll20-character-sheets/blob/master/D%26D_3-5/charsheet_3-5.html) on 2026-10-18. Its overall Arcane Spell Failure input is separate from the armor and shield component inputs. Buzzer's supplied export records 15 in the overall input and 15 on the armor, not two additive penalties.

## Open evidence — not a completion certificate

- **Skill provenance still requires player/DM review where the source is ambiguous.** Numeric source totals now get a separate one-time automatic adjustment; nonnumeric misc/feat overlap is presented under Skills > Imported bonus review, without guessing. The owner confirmed Buzzer's Hide/Move Silently misc +4 includes racial +2 and Stealthy +2: choose Already included in imported misc for those two skills. Original-export checks then become 13 and 8, while later ranks still contribute. Intimidate 9 to 11 is NOT established as an import bug: the source has no numeric total or misc bonus, and five Bluff ranks legitimately grant +2 [synergy](https://www.d20srd.org/srd/skills/intimidate.htm). Do not suppress this bonus just to reproduce an incomplete manual total. Racial and conditional bonus provenance remains part of the broader audit.
- **Weapon modifier reconciliation still needs coverage.** Recorded dice, explicit Focus/Specialization double counting, explicit enhancement stacking, and literal global weapon modifiers are repaired for new and existing imports. Proficiency, ability selectors, other implicit modifiers in numeric attack totals, and bonuses embedded in complete damage formulas still need auditing. Focus overlap in numeric totals is covered by the version-two weapon-bonus repair. Do not assume a full formula's internal bonus types merely from its final number. Enhancement/global repairs conservatively require unchanged relevant components; edited entries remain flagged for review rather than being overwritten. Global modifier types are unspecified in the source, so the UI warns not to duplicate Active effects or feats. The source Action tab's global enabled flag is honored; legacy custom macros can intentionally disagree with that flag and need player review.
- **Source errors versus importer errors must remain distinct.** For example, the supplied source explicitly records a Reflex base of 2 and custom studded-leather armor bonus/max-Dex values. Do not silently replace these with standard values. Surface discrepancies and preserve player/DM choices.
- Continue checking caster progression, prepared/spent spell counts, temporary bonuses, resource usage, racial contributions, carrying weight, and fixed/repeating aliases against the actual exporter and upstream sheet. Existing passing tests do not prove exhaustive coverage.
- Shield audit follow-up: nonproficiency penalties are calculated for weapon attacks and selected physical skills, but bare Strength/Dexterity checks (including initiative) and Weapon Finesse's shield ACP still need dedicated regression coverage and reconciliation with source totals. Do not silently add a penalty already embedded in an imported numeric total.

The weapon component fields were checked against the upstream Roll20 sheet on 2026-10-20: weapon focus contributes to attack, specialization to damage, and enhancement to both. A Fighter 4 test with Strength 16 and both feats now stays at +8 attack / 1d8+5 after activation instead of gaining a second +1/+2. A level-12 existing-save test retains later Greater feats, Strength gains, weapon enhancement and extra damage while removing only the original duplicated feat components.

## Verification expectations

For each repair, verify a fresh import, automatic adoption, repeated browser/native save-load, and a pre-fix save with later advancement. Check unchanged source JSON, character identity, HP/resource expenditure, ranks/history, notes, and unrelated edits. Exercise replacement equipment, user overrides, explicit zeroes, omitted fields, and unresolved formulas. Never execute formulas or instructions from imported data.

Keep Buzzer's original export outside the repository. Use minimal non-identifying regression fixtures in committed tests. Live Beyond20 delivery remains an external acceptance check; local dice and generated Roll20 macros do not prove delivery to a game.
